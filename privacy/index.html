PRIVACY NOTICE · NOVEMBER 24, 2026

Your messages stay private.

Optional visitor statistics

With your permission, Google Analytics 4 measures visits, page views, device categories, approximate location and referral sources. Analytics cookies are only loaded after you choose Allow statistics. No thanks keeps analytics off. You can change your choice using Statistics preferences at the bottom of any page. Your choice is saved in this browser.

We disable advertising signals and enhanced automatic interaction measurement. Enquiry text, chatbot searches, names and email addresses are not sent to Analytics. Page addresses and referring addresses omit query strings and fragments. Google may process technical usage data outside your country under its privacy policy. Statistics are aggregate estimates and may omit visitors who decline or block analytics.

Website guide chatbot

The guide finder searches this website in your browser. Its conversation is not sent to an AI service or stored by this website. Messages disappear when you leave or reload the page. If you choose Send an enquiry, the separate form sends only the name, email, topic, message, consent and page address you submit through FormSubmit to Seoul Picked. Guide conversation text is not automatically attached.

Contact enquiries

The contact form collects your name, email address, topic, subject, message and consent so Seoul Picked can read and respond to your enquiry. Messages are not published as public posts or comments on this website.

How a message is delivered

Form submissions are sent to FormSubmit, a third-party form delivery service, and forwarded to the Seoul Picked mailbox at wrpdla@naver.com, hosted by Naver Mail. Those services may process messages outside your country and handle technical information under their own policies. FormSubmit states that it retains submissions for 30 days. Email copies may remain in the recipient mailbox after that period.

You can review FormSubmit's privacy policy. The form may present a Google reCAPTCHA check to prevent spam.

Your choices

Submitting the form is optional. Please send only information needed for your enquiry. To request deletion of an enquiry held by Seoul Picked, contact wrpdla@naver.com from the address used to submit it. We will review your request; copies retained by external providers are governed by their policies.

Embedded videos and third-party links

Some articles embed YouTube videos using the youtube-nocookie.com player. Loading or playing a video can connect your browser to YouTube and Google. External sites apply their own privacy policies. This site also loads video thumbnails from YouTube and fonts from Google Fonts.
