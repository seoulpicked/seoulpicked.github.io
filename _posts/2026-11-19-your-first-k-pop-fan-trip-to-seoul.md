## Decide what matters most
Your trip might center on a concert, an album store, an exhibition or a themed café. These experiences have different booking requirements.

## Verify official schedules
Check artists' official channels and ticketing platforms before booking travel. Unofficial social posts can be outdated or inaccurate.

## Build a flexible day
Combine music-related stops with nearby cafés, museums or shopping areas. Confirm business hours directly.

## Be a considerate fan
Respect privacy, local rules and any photography restrictions. Avoid visiting private residences or non-public workplaces.
