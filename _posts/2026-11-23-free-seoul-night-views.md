A Seoul evening does not have to begin with a ticket. Our Short introduces three outdoor settings: **Cheonggyecheon**, **Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP)** and **the Han River**. Watch the video above for a quick preview, then choose the setting that fits your evening.

## Cheonggyecheon: begin with a stream walk
Choose the stream if you want a walking-focused stop. Decide where you will enter and leave before heading down, especially if you are meeting someone or catching a train afterward. Check posted notices and weather conditions; access to waterside paths can be restricted for safety.

## DDP: choose an outdoor architecture stop
Make DDP your pick if the architecture is what caught your eye in the video. This suggestion concerns the outdoor setting. Do not assume that an exhibition, indoor venue or special event has the same admission arrangements as the public space around it.

## The Han River: leave room for a slower evening
Pick a riverside stop that works with your transport home. Rather than trying to cover every place in one evening, allow time to enjoy one location and check the route back before you arrive.

## Plan around conditions, not a promise of lights
Lighting schedules, events and path access can change. Check the current location notices before travelling, dress for the weather, and follow any closures. The Short is an introduction to places, not a guarantee that every light installation or activity will be operating when you visit.
