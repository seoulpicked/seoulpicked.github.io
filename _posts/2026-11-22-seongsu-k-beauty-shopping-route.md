Our beauty Short compares three ways to shop: discovering brands at Musinsa Beauty, exploring the broad assortment at Olive Young N Seongsu, and checking low-priced beauty basics at Daiso. The useful question is what you need from each stop, not which has the most dramatic queue.

## Musinsa Beauty Seongsu: make a discovery list
The video introduces the store at **87 Yeonmujang-gil**, with independent beauty brands and a substantial fragrance selection. Start with a category you want to compare—such as lip products or fragrance—rather than trying everything at once.

For fragrance, narrow down a few options on test strips before trying a favourite on skin. For skincare, compare the actual ingredient list and directions. A popular shelf or a long opening-day queue is not evidence that a product will suit you.

## Olive Young N: separate shopping from appointments
The Short's second stop is **13 Yeonmujang 7-gil**. It highlights both retail shopping and beauty services. A store being open does not mean a scan, consultation or other service is immediately available; check the current booking and eligibility details for the specific service.

Bring a short list of products or ingredients you already use. That makes it easier to compare alternatives without turning one visit into a complete routine overhaul.

## Daiso near Konkuk University: check the essentials
The last stop is the beauty-focused Daiso featured in the Short. It is a useful comparison for inexpensive tools, puffs, masks and makeup basics. Read the individual label rather than assuming a familiar-looking package matches a more expensive product.

## Suggested route
The video groups Musinsa and Olive Young in Seongsu, then continues one stop on Line 2 to Konkuk University. Check the exact branch location before departing. Prices, services and stock vary, and the opening-day statistics in the video are historical context rather than a prediction of your own visit.
