Not every pancake needs syrup. **Haemul pajeon (해물파전)** is a savory Korean seafood-and-scallion pancake. Our Short follows the action from preparing scallions to mixing batter, adding seafood to the pan, flipping and serving.

## Watch the change, not the clock
The video compresses the cooking process into about thirty seconds. It is a food-culture introduction, not a timed or measured recipe. The edited sequence should not be used to judge cooking time or whether seafood is cooked through.

## From ingredients to a finished dish
Scallions and batter are combined, seafood goes into the pan, and the mixture is spread out before the pancake is turned. The finished pancake is served on a plate.

The footage comes from **Taste Korea: Haemul Pajeon**, produced by AFN Humphreys and credited to Emily Dawson, Keaton Habeck and Hana Pong. It was filmed in Pyeongtaek in 2021; this is not a review of a current Seoul restaurant. The English narration is synthetic and the edit, captions and instrumental music were prepared for Seoul Picked.
