## Why Seongsu?
Seongsu is known for a mix of former industrial spaces, cafés, independent retail and changing pop-up experiences. Specific shops and events can change quickly.

## Morning: start slowly
Arrive by public transport and explore the surrounding streets. Pick a café based on current opening hours and your preferences.

## Afternoon: browse and wander
Look for design shops, fashion boutiques or temporary exhibitions. If a pop-up is important to you, confirm its dates and entry rules first.

## Evening: keep it flexible
Choose dinner nearby or return to another part of Seoul. Use a current navigation app for transit and walking routes.
