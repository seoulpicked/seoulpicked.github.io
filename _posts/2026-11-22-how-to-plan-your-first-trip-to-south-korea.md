## Start with the basics
Choose your travel dates, check your passport validity and confirm entry requirements for your nationality. Requirements vary and may change, so use the official [Korea Visa Portal](https://www.visa.go.kr/).

## Pick a base
Seoul is a useful starting point for shopping, food and cultural attractions. When comparing places to stay, check the distance to a subway station and the routes to your top attractions.

## Build a flexible itinerary
Group nearby neighborhoods together rather than crossing the city several times each day. Save a few indoor options in case of rain or extreme weather.

## Plan the practical details
Check payment options, mobile data access and transport information before departure. The official [VISITKOREA](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/) site is a useful reference.

## Leave space for surprises
Choose one or two priorities each day and leave time for spontaneous discoveries.
