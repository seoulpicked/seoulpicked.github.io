Our Jamsil Short puts two stops together: an outdoor Christmas market around Lotte World Tower and a Godiva mochi stop inside Lotte World Mall. They suit different parts of the day, but both work better when you check their entry or pickup arrangements first.

## The market: go for the setting
The video presents the Lotte Town Christmas Market as a British-street-inspired setting with a clock tower, a large tree, festive food and a two-storey carousel. The visual appeal is the reason to allow time to wander rather than treating it as a single photo stop.

The upload describes the **2026–27 winter season** and a choice between free general admission and paid priority entry. Free does not automatically mean an unrestricted walk-in: timed reservations, queues and sold-out slots can still apply. Check the organizer's current booking notice for the date you want.

## Godiva mochi: arrange pickup before the detour
The second stop in the video is **Lotte World Mall B1**. The mochi combines a chewy outer layer with chocolate and fillings such as fruit or gelato.

The Short describes same-day CatchTable booking, purchase limits and a pickup window. These details can change, so use the current listing rather than relying on an old alarm time. Make sure you can reach the shop within the pickup window before joining another queue.

## Put the two together
A practical order is to resolve the dessert booking first, then choose a market slot that leaves enough time for pickup and the walk between stops. Leave the outdoor part for later if you want the lights, and plan an indoor break if the weather is cold.

Jamsil Station on Lines 2 and 8 is the transit starting point featured in the video. Search for the specific market entrance and the mall shop separately; the station complex and shopping area are large.
