## Pick by the experience you want
**The tangerine jelly** is the fruit-led option: the description identifies a peeled whole tangerine inside a cup of jelly. A spoon makes more sense than trying to drink it.

**The Bonjuk cake** plays with packaging. It comes in a porridge-style bowl, while the contents are chocolate cake. Read the product name instead of relying on what the container suggests.

**The book-cover breads** are a collection, not one flavour. The video description lists milk cream, sweet-potato cream, butter cinnamon and red-bean options. Choose the filling first; the literary cover is part of the presentation.

## Before you buy
Check the expiry date and storage instructions. Read the allergen label, especially for dairy, egg, wheat or nuts, and do not assume every item in a collection has the same ingredients. For a small dessert crawl, visit the three chains in one neighbourhood and share only what you can store and eat appropriately.
