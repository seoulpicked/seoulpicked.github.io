## Why visit a convenience store?
Korean convenience stores can be an easy way to explore everyday snacks and drinks without planning a full restaurant visit. Selection varies by location and season.

## What to browse
Look for packaged seaweed snacks, rice crackers, instant noodles, ready-to-drink teas and seasonal sweets. Product labels can help you compare flavors and ingredients.

## Check dietary details
If you have allergies or dietary restrictions, read the ingredient label carefully. English translations on packaging may not cover every detail.

## A simple tasting plan
Pick one savory snack, one sweet snack and a drink. Compare the flavors and keep a note of what you enjoyed. Prices and stock differ by store.
