A K-pop day in Seoul does not have to revolve around a concert. Our Short brings together three spaces with different purposes: filming your own clip, browsing an entertainment company's flagship shop, and exploring a dedicated K-pop retail space.

## HiKR Ground: make something of your own
At **40 Cheonggyecheon-ro**, HiKR Ground is the interactive stop. The video focuses on media walls and music-video-style sets where visitors can experiment with a song, lighting and props.

Allow time for the experience you actually want to try. Entry and access to a popular set are different things: a free venue can still have a queue or temporarily unavailable areas. Check the current visitor notice, including closing days, before travelling across town.

## KWANGYA@SEOUL: an SM-focused visit
The second stop is at **83-21 Wangsimni-ro**, in the Seoul Forest area. The Short highlights the media-led interior and official albums, lightsticks and merchandise.

This is a shop as well as a fan destination. Browsing is different from buying a product or using a paid photo booth. Set a shopping budget before entering if you are collecting for several artists.

## K-POP SQUARE Hongdae: browse the changing lineup
At **141 Yanghwa-ro**, the video introduces a K-pop store with artist pop-ups, character goods, photo cards and photo opportunities. The appeal depends partly on which artist or display is featured when you visit, so check the latest lineup rather than assuming the one in the video is permanent.

## Plan by neighbourhood
These three stops are not one short walking route. HiKR Ground belongs with a central-Seoul day; KWANGYA fits Seoul Forest and Seongsu; K-POP SQUARE fits Hongdae. Choosing the one that matches your existing itinerary can save more time than trying to collect all three in one afternoon.

The Short describes free general entry. Merchandise, capsule toys, photo booths and special experiences may carry separate charges or entry rules.
