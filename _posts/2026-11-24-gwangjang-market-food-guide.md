Our new ten-second Short introduces **Gwangjang Market (광장시장)** through three very different bites: a hot mung-bean pancake, seasoned raw beef and small seaweed rice rolls. Use the loop as an introduction, then decide what you actually want to order.

## Choose a dish, then check the stall menu
| Dish | What to expect | Indicative price quoted in our video description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bindaetteok (빈대떡) | Mung-bean pancake cooked on a griddle | Around ₩5,000 for a basic piece |
| Yukhoe (육회) | Seasoned raw beef, often with egg yolk | Around ₩20,000–23,000 a plate |
| Mayak gimbap (마약김밥) | Small gimbap with a mustard-style dipping sauce | Around ₩3,000; check the portion size |

These are examples from the Short's November 2026 description, not a live menu or a promise that every stall charges the same. Ask the price and portion size before ordering. Yukhoe is a raw dish; choose a cooked option if raw meat or egg is unsuitable for you.

## A practical first visit
The market address in our guide is **88 Changgyeonggung-ro, Jongno-gu, Seoul**. Jongno 5-ga Station on Line 1 and Euljiro 4-ga on Lines 2 and 5 are the nearby subway options mentioned in the video description.

Start with one cooked dish, add a smaller snack if you are still hungry, and check whether the stall accepts your card before sitting down. The video's suggested ₩15,000–20,000 per-person snack budget will not cover every combination, especially a whole yukhoe plate ordered alone.

## Check the part of the market you need
The food alley and the market's other shops keep different schedules. Our description lists the food alley as 09:00–23:00, but individual stalls can open later or close earlier. Confirm the stall you want before making a special trip.
