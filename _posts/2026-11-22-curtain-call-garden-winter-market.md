You do not need a full evening to enjoy a winter market in central Seoul. Curtain Call Garden fills the garden behind the Sejong Center for the Performing Arts with warm snacks, craft stalls and seasonal lights. Our video maps out a compact stop you can fit around a walk through Gwanghwamun.

## The essentials
The winter season runs **November 17, 2026–January 3, 2027**, daily **11:00–21:00**, at the Sejong Center's Arts Garden. Admission is free and no reservation is required; food and shopping cost extra. These details are confirmed by Seoul's official festival listing and city announcement, checked November 22, 2026.

## What to eat and browse
The video highlights bungeoppang, hotteok, roasted sweet potatoes and fish-cake skewers, plus warm mulled wine. Try one warm snack before browsing the craft stalls. The vendor selection rotates, so the same items may not be available on every visit.

Knits, jewellery and ceramics make this more of a browse-and-snack stop than a large shopping excursion. The seasonal Santa photo area is another reason to pause, especially after the lights come on.

## Find the garden, not just the front door
Search for **Sejong Center, 175 Sejong-daero**, and look for the garden behind the performance hall. The video approaches from Gwanghwamun Station on Line 5; allow extra time to walk around the building rather than stopping at the main entrance.

## Our suggested evening
Arrive before dusk, choose something hot, browse the market, then continue toward Gwanghwamun Square. Around an hour is a reasonable starting plan. Dress for an outdoor visit even where heaters are provided, and check the organizer's updates if rain or severe weather is forecast.

**Official visitor information:** [FUN SEOUL festival listing](https://festival.seoul.go.kr/festival/main/festivalView.do?festacode=987) · [Seoul city announcement](https://mediahub.seoul.go.kr/archives/2020086).
