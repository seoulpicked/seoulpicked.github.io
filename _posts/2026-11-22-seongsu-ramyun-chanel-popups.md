One of Seongsu's attractions is how quickly a street can change. Our Short pairs two contrasting brand experiences: a ramyun-focused visit and a winter-themed Chanel pop-up. This is a guide to the experiences featured in the video, rather than a promise that either installation will remain open indefinitely.

## Shin Ramyun Bunsik: build the bowl
The video places the Shin Ramyun experience at **52 Seongsu-ilro 4-gil**. Its main food idea is a customized bowl: choose toppings and turn a familiar packet of noodles into a more personal lunch stop.

The upload describes a paid ramyun experience alongside free admission. Those are separate costs. Before queueing, check the menu, what is included in the experience, and whether the activity floor closes earlier than the venue itself.

## Chanel: a different kind of winter stop
The second location in the Short is **9 Seongsu-ro 7ga-gil**. The video describes a chalet-inspired holiday setting, photo areas and seasonal beauty products.

Think of this as a visual and browsing stop rather than a scheduled performance. Walk-in registration and advance booking can operate differently, and promotional gifts or special activities may have additional conditions. Do not assume free admission includes every experience or product.

## Check the dates before building your route
Both are seasonal or promotional experiences. The Short's description includes planned end dates, but we have not independently reconfirmed those schedules for this article. Check the brands' current announcements before making a special trip; an older video can remain online after a pop-up ends.

## Keep the walk useful
If both are operating on your visit date, check the registration situation at your priority stop first and use the waiting time for the other only when the return rules allow it. If one has closed, keep the rest of the day centred on nearby cafés and shops rather than crossing Seoul for an uncertain queue.
