## Start with the bowl
Bibimbap brings rice, vegetables and other toppings together for you to mix. The photograph shows one restaurant presentation; ingredients and portions differ, so it is not a menu for a particular Seoul restaurant.

## Ask before adding sauce
Gochujang can add heat and sweetness. Add a little, mix, then taste before adding more. Ask the restaurant if the sauce is already mixed in.

## Choose what suits you
Some versions include beef or egg, while others put vegetables at the centre. A vegetable-looking bowl is not automatically vegetarian: ask about the sauce, stock and toppings. Tell staff about allergies before ordering.

## A useful first order
Read the menu for whether the dish comes in an ordinary bowl or a hot stone bowl. If the bowl is hot, keep your hands clear and follow the restaurant's guidance. Compare the complete dish and price rather than assuming every bibimbap is the same.
