Our hanbok Short links three palace visits: **Gyeongbokgung, Changdeokgung and Deoksugung**. The useful distinction is between free palace admission for an eligible outfit and the separate cost of renting that outfit.

## Wear the full outfit
The Royal Palaces and Tombs Center accepts traditional and modernized hanbok that follow its guidelines. You need both the upper garment, **jeogori**, and a skirt or trousers. A hanbok top worn with jeans, a skirt with an ordinary T-shirt, or an outer coat alone does not qualify.

Before paying a rental shop, ask whether the chosen outfit meets the palace rules. The shop's rental fee, styling extras, deposit and return deadline are separate from admission.

## Choose a palace that fits your day
| Palace from the Short | A useful planning distinction |
| --- | --- |
| Gyeongbokgung | Start here if this is your main palace visit; verify the day's hours and closure notice |
| Changdeokgung | General palace admission and the Secret Garden are different tickets |
| Deoksugung | Check the current evening schedule if you want a later visit |

Hanbok does **not** remove the separate Secret Garden ticket requirement at Changdeokgung. Its timed-entry availability also needs to be checked independently.

## Avoid an overpacked rental window
The video's one-day route is an idea, not a timed itinerary. Changing, styling, walking between palaces and returning the outfit all use rental time. Pick one main palace first, then add another only if your rental duration and the day's opening hours comfortably allow it. Do not assume one day's closures apply to every palace.
