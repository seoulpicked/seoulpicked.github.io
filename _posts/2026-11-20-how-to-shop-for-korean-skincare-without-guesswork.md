## Begin with your own needs
Start by identifying whether you are looking for a cleanser, moisturizer or sunscreen. Popularity alone does not make a product suitable for your skin.

## Read the packaging
Compare ingredient lists, directions and the product's intended use. If you have sensitive skin, introduce one new product at a time and consider a patch test.

## Compare before buying
Korean beauty retailers carry a wide range of brands, but availability and prices change. Check official product listings for the latest details.

## Keep your routine simple
A basic routine is often easier to evaluate than adding many new products at once. Sunscreen should be used according to its labeled directions.
